      How it works

      The reasons behind every pick.

      Every line has the same chance of winning. AI Draw changes what can be changed, and shows its evidence. Open any panel for the detail; the figures follow the game you chose above.

      1 · Fewer people to share withAvoids the numbers and patterns other players pick most, measured from official winner counts.
      2 · Lines that work togetherYour lines are spread so they rarely win in the same draw, raising the chance that at least one wins.
      3 · A budget that holdsShows what fits, the real chance of getting money back, and when a ticket is worth most.

      What it will never do

      The lines we do not cross.

      Predict a drawDraws are random. We tested every strategy on 13 years of results and say so plainly.
      Sell tickets or take moneyNo betting account, no payments, no automatic purchases. You buy from your licensed lottery, if at all.
      Push you to play moreNo streaks, no "due" numbers, no loss-chasing prompts. The cap only ever stops you.
      Send your data anywhereIt runs on your device. Your lines and spending stay with you.
      Hide the oddsEvery screen that generates a line shows its jackpot chance: 1 in 139,838,160.
      Serve under-18sLottery products are 18+ in Germany and across Europe. AI Draw is too.
