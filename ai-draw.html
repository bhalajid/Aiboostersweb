AI Draw analyses the full archive of official Eurojackpot results since 2012, gives you one reproducible line per draw, steers you away from numbers other players over-pick, and holds your spending to a fixed cap. It does not predict draws. Nothing can.

The gamesEurojackpot: 5 numbers from 1–50 and 2 Euro numbers from 1–12, drawn every Tuesday and Friday, played across Europe.
Lotto 6aus49: 6 numbers from 1–49 plus a Superzahl from your ticket, drawn every Wednesday and Saturday in Germany.

      What it does

      Four things, done properly.

      01

      Every Eurojackpot result

      The complete archive, checked draw by draw for gaps, bad dates and impossible numbers, including the two times the game's rules changed.

      02

      One line per draw

      Each line comes from a recorded seed and data version, so you can regenerate it exactly. Stored lines can never be quietly changed.

      03

      Fewer people to share with

      Every prize is split between its winners. AI Draw measures which numbers players over-pick and avoids them. Your odds stay the same.

      04

      A cap that holds

      A fixed monthly limit, with lines generated for free and only tickets you actually bought counted against it. It never suggests chasing losses.
