Privacy policy

What we collect on aiboosters.guru and in AI Draw, why, where it is kept, and how you get it deleted. In plain words, without tracking, ads or selling data.

In force from 16 November 2026

Who is responsible The short version Visiting this website Contacting us AI Draw Service providers Your rights Changes

1. Who is responsible

The controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is AIBoosters Consulting, Balaji Durai. Email: balaji@aiboosters.guru. Write to this address for every privacy question or request.

2. The short version

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, no profiling. We do not sell or share your data for marketing.
  • AI Draw works without an account. Then your tickets and settings stay in your browser and never reach us.
  • If you create an AI Draw account, we store your email address and the tickets you save, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany. You can delete the account and every ticket yourself, at any time, in one click.

3. Visiting this website

Server logs. When you open a page, our host Vercel processes your IP address, the time, the page requested, the referring page and your browser’s user-agent, to deliver the page and to protect the service against abuse. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). These logs are kept for a short period by Vercel and are not combined with other data.

Fonts. Our fonts are hosted on this website itself. Your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.

Program libraries. To run the 3D graphics and the AI Draw account feature, your browser loads program libraries from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. For this, your IP address and browser information reach those providers. They set no cookies for us and we receive no data from them. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (reliable, fast delivery of the site).

Preview assistant. The chat panel on the main pages is a scripted preview that runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type in it is sent anywhere.

Cookies. We set no cookies. AI Draw saves data in your browser’s local storage, described in section 5.

4. Contacting us

The contact page opens your own email program; nothing is sent through this website. When you email us, we use your address and message to answer and, if it comes to it, to prepare and run an engagement. Legal basis: steps before a contract and the contract itself (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), otherwise our legitimate interest in replying (Art. 6(1)(f)). We delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed, unless tax or commercial law requires us to keep it longer.

5. AI Draw

AI Draw is for adults (18+). It works in two ways:

Without an account

Your saved tickets and your settings (country, budget, ticket fee) are kept in your browser’s local storage on your device. They are not sent to us. You remove them by deleting the ticket in AI Draw or by clearing your browser’s site data. Draw results come from a file on our own website; your browser does not contact any lottery operator.

With an account

You can sign in with your email address to keep your tickets on all your devices. There is no password: we send you a one-time sign-in link.

WhatWhy
Email addressTo send the sign-in link and identify your account
Tickets you save: game, draw date, numbers, SuperzahlTo show your tickets and check them against the official results
Account dates (created, last sign-in) and technical sign-in records including IP addressTo run the sign-in securely and prevent abuse
A sign-in token in your browser’s local storageTo keep you signed in on that device until you sign out

Legal basis. Providing the account you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); for the security records, our legitimate interest in protecting accounts (Art. 6(1)(f)). Storing the sign-in token on your device is strictly necessary for the service you requested.

Where. Accounts and tickets are stored by our provider Supabase in its Frankfurt, Germany (EU) region. Every account can read and change only its own tickets; this is enforced by the database itself.

How long. Until you delete them. If you sign in on a device that already holds tickets, those tickets are moved into your account.

Delete everything. Signed in, open My tickets and choose “Delete my account”. Your account and all its tickets are deleted at once. Copies in the provider’s backups are overwritten in the normal backup cycle. You can also ask us by email.

No decisions about you. AI Draw does not profile you or make automated decisions with legal or similar effects. Its number suggestions are statistics for entertainment and never increase your chance of winning.

6. Service providers

We use these providers, each bound by a data processing agreement or acting under its own privacy terms:

  • Vercel Inc., USA: website hosting and delivery.
  • Supabase Inc., USA: AI Draw accounts, database and sign-in emails; data stored in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Cloudflare Inc. (cdnjs) and jsDelivr: delivery of program libraries.

Where a provider can access data from outside the EU/EEA, the transfer is based on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, otherwise on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Email us and we answer within one month. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one where you live or work.

8. Changes

If we change how we handle data, we update this page and the date at the top. For changes that affect AI Draw accounts, we tell you on the AI Draw page before they apply.
